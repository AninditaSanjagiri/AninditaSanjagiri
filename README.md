### `01` `about.me`

I'm a third-year engineering student who builds ML systems that touch real data and real constraints — not just notebooks that stop at a validation score. Most of my work sits across NLP, computer vision, and applied security: multi-model pipelines, honest evaluation, and shipping something that actually runs.

I spent a summer at **Bajaj Finance** deploying speech-to-text pipelines on Databricks and training risk-classification models for underwriting — which is where I got interested in the gap between "the model works" and "the system works." That's mostly what I optimize for now: clear baselines, explainable predictions, and code that survives contact with production.

Currently deep in PyTorch, vision transformers, and reproducing papers I find interesting rather than just reading them.
